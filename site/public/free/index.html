Get your hands off my mouse.

DuoSeat gives the person next to you their own cursor and keyboard on your Windows PC. You keep yours. You both work at the same time, in different windows, and nobody's typing lands in the wrong place.

Download DuoSeat.exe130 KB · Windows 10/11 · no installer
1. Same Wi-FiRun the same .exe on both laptops. Yours is Player A, theirs is Player B.
2. Type the PINB clicks Find, types the 4 digits on your screen, Connect.
3. Ctrl+Alt+XTheir mouse and keys now drive the orange cursor. Yours didn't move.
No second laptop?Any phone works from the browser. No TV? It makes a screen. Far away? It makes a link.
