DuoSeat

Two people, one PC. Each gets their own cursor and keyboard.

GitHub Download

Hand someone a cursor, not your laptop.

Your friend sits next to you with their own laptop. DuoSeat turns their mouse and keyboard into a second, orange cursor on your PC. You keep yours. Both of you type at the same time and nothing lands in the wrong window.
